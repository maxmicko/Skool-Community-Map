---
name: Skool Map product tone
description: Durable UX and messaging direction for the Skool Community Map and private Radar.
---

The public Community Map should feel like a neutral, community-maintained open-data project: transparent, documented, useful, and free of sales language. The private Radar can be denser and more personal, but should feel like a quiet research console that distinguishes observed facts from interpretation and cites public sources.

**Why:** The product earns trust by organizing the user's ecosystem rather than immediately pitching a service. A salesy public layer would undermine the curiosity and self-location loop.

**How to apply:** Keep map/directory CTAs utility-based ("Search your community", "Suggest an update", "Follow this category"). Reserve competitor intelligence, alerts, and market recommendations for the private Radar experience.
